## Semantic layer
Rules:
- datum_key and zeit_key are local (Europe/Berlin); ts_utc is UTC. Years, months, days, weeks and hours in a question are local unless it says UTC. A local day has 96 quarter-hours, 92 on the spring clock change and 100 on the autumn clock change.
- fact_erzeugung and fact_last hold energy in MWh per 15-minute interval; fact_preis holds one row per hour (EUR/MWh). Mean power in MW = AVG(MWh per quarter-hour) * 4.
- Hourly price together with hourly load: rpt.v_preis_last_stunde (hourly load = sum of the four quarter-hours).
- fact_kapazitaet has one row per month, Bundesland and energy carrier; datum_key is the first day of the month and bestand_mw is the stock at the end of that month. Germany = sum over all Bundeslaender including AWZ (offshore zone) and Unbekannt (unknown).
- Pumpspeicher counts as Konventionell. Pumping consumption is fact_last.pumpspeicher_verbrauch_mwh, not generation.
- Kernenergie generation ends on 2023-04-15; later rows are NULL or 0.
- Only the bidding zone DE-LU has facts. The control areas 50Hertz, Amprion, TenneT and TransnetBW exist in dw.dim_region without facts.
- Units: GWh = MWh / 1000, TWh = MWh / 1000000, GW = MW / 1000; percent = 100 * share.

Measures (name DE / EN [unit]: T-SQL template; placeholders in braces):

- Erzeugung Erneuerbar (MWh) / Renewable generation (MWh) [MWh]: Generation of the category Erneuerbar. SQL: SELECT SUM(f.erzeugung_mwh) FROM dw.fact_erzeugung f JOIN dw.dim_energietraeger e ON e.energietraeger_key = f.energietraeger_key WHERE e.kategorie_de = N'Erneuerbar' AND f.datum_key BETWEEN {von} AND {bis}
- EE-Anteil % / Renewable share % [percent]: Renewable generation divided by total generation, times 100. SQL: SELECT 100.0 * SUM(CASE WHEN e.kategorie_de = N'Erneuerbar' THEN f.erzeugung_mwh END) / SUM(f.erzeugung_mwh) FROM dw.fact_erzeugung f JOIN dw.dim_energietraeger e ON e.energietraeger_key = f.energietraeger_key WHERE f.datum_key BETWEEN {von} AND {bis}
- Netzlast (MWh) / Grid load (MWh) [MWh]: Sum of grid load. SQL: SELECT SUM(l.netzlast_mwh) FROM dw.fact_last l WHERE l.datum_key BETWEEN {von} AND {bis}
- Residuallast (MWh) / Residual load (MWh) [MWh]: Sum of residual load (grid load minus wind and PV). SQL: SELECT SUM(l.residuallast_mwh) FROM dw.fact_last l WHERE l.datum_key BETWEEN {von} AND {bis}
- Residuallast Minimum (MWh/15 min) / Residual load minimum (MWh/15 min) [MWh per quarter-hour]: Smallest quarter-hour value of the residual load. SQL: SELECT MIN(l.residuallast_mwh) FROM dw.fact_last l WHERE l.datum_key BETWEEN {von} AND {bis}

- Day-Ahead Ø (€/MWh) / Day-ahead average (EUR/MWh) [EUR/MWh]: Average hourly day-ahead price. SQL: SELECT AVG(p.day_ahead_eur_mwh) FROM dw.fact_preis p WHERE p.datum_key BETWEEN {von} AND {bis}

- Negative Preisstunden / Negative price hours [hours]: Number of hours with a day-ahead price below 0. SQL: SELECT COUNT(*) FROM dw.fact_preis p WHERE p.day_ahead_eur_mwh < 0 AND p.datum_key BETWEEN {von} AND {bis}
- Erzeugung Vorjahr (MWh) / Generation previous year (MWh) [MWh]: Generation in the same period one year earlier. SQL: SELECT SUM(f.erzeugung_mwh) FROM dw.fact_erzeugung f WHERE f.datum_key BETWEEN {von_vorjahr} AND {bis_vorjahr}
- Erzeugung Δ Vorjahr % / Generation change vs previous year % [percent]: Change of generation against the same period one year earlier, in percent. SQL: SELECT 100.0 * (a.cur - b.prev) / b.prev FROM (SELECT SUM(erzeugung_mwh) AS cur FROM dw.fact_erzeugung WHERE datum_key BETWEEN {von} AND {bis}) a CROSS JOIN (SELECT SUM(erzeugung_mwh) AS prev FROM dw.fact_erzeugung WHERE datum_key BETWEEN {von_vorjahr} AND {bis_vorjahr}) b
- EE-Anteil Vorjahr % / Renewable share previous year % [percent]: Renewable share in the same period one year earlier. SQL: SELECT 100.0 * SUM(CASE WHEN e.kategorie_de = N'Erneuerbar' THEN f.erzeugung_mwh END) / SUM(f.erzeugung_mwh) FROM dw.fact_erzeugung f JOIN dw.dim_energietraeger e ON e.energietraeger_key = f.energietraeger_key WHERE f.datum_key BETWEEN {von_vorjahr} AND {bis_vorjahr}
- EE-Anteil Δ Vorjahr (pp) / Renewable share change vs previous year (pp) [percentage points]: Renewable share minus the share one year earlier. SQL: SELECT a.anteil - b.anteil FROM (SELECT 100.0 * SUM(CASE WHEN e.kategorie_de = N'Erneuerbar' THEN f.erzeugung_mwh END) / SUM(f.erzeugung_mwh) AS anteil FROM dw.fact_erzeugung f JOIN dw.dim_energietraeger e ON e.energietraeger_key = f.energietraeger_key WHERE f.datum_key BETWEEN {von} AND {bis}) a CROSS JOIN (SELECT 100.0 * SUM(CASE WHEN e.kategorie_de = N'Erneuerbar' THEN f.erzeugung_mwh END) / SUM(f.erzeugung_mwh) AS anteil FROM dw.fact_erzeugung f JOIN dw.dim_energietraeger e ON e.energietraeger_key = f.energietraeger_key WHERE f.datum_key BETWEEN {von_vorjahr} AND {bis_vorjahr}) b
- Netzlast Vormonat (MWh) / Grid load previous month (MWh) [MWh]: Grid load of the previous month. SQL: SELECT SUM(l.netzlast_mwh) FROM dw.fact_last l JOIN dw.dim_datum d ON d.datum_key = l.datum_key WHERE d.jahr_monat = '{vormonat}'
- Netzlast Δ Vormonat % / Grid load change vs previous month % [percent]: Change of the monthly grid load against the previous month, in percent. SQL: SELECT 100.0 * (a.mwh - b.mwh) / b.mwh FROM (SELECT SUM(l.netzlast_mwh) AS mwh FROM dw.fact_last l JOIN dw.dim_datum d ON d.datum_key = l.datum_key WHERE d.jahr_monat = '{monat}') a CROSS JOIN (SELECT SUM(l.netzlast_mwh) AS mwh FROM dw.fact_last l JOIN dw.dim_datum d ON d.datum_key = l.datum_key WHERE d.jahr_monat = '{vormonat}') b
- Zubau (MW) / Capacity additions (MW) [MW]: Capacity commissioned in the months of the period. SQL: SELECT SUM(k.zubau_mw) FROM dw.fact_kapazitaet k WHERE k.datum_key BETWEEN {von} AND {bis}
- Zubau YTD (MW) / Capacity additions year to date (MW) [MW]: Additions from January of the year up to and including the given month. SQL: SELECT SUM(k.zubau_mw) FROM dw.fact_kapazitaet k WHERE k.datum_key BETWEEN {jahr}0101 AND {monatserster}
- Installierte Leistung (MW) / Installed capacity (MW) [MW]: Stock at the end of the last month with capacity data on or before the end of the period (equals the cumulative anfangsbestand_mw + zubau_mw - abgang_mw); never a sum over several months. Without a period end, use the newest month. SQL: SELECT SUM(k.bestand_mw) FROM dw.fact_kapazitaet k WHERE k.datum_key = (SELECT MAX(k2.datum_key) FROM dw.fact_kapazitaet k2 WHERE k2.datum_key <= {bis})
- Volllaststunden (h) / Full-load hours (h) [hours]: Generation of a carrier divided by the average month-end stock of the months from the month of the first to the last generation day in the period (months without capacity rows do not count; not extrapolated for incomplete years). SQL: SELECT g.mwh / c.mw FROM (SELECT SUM(f.erzeugung_mwh) AS mwh, MIN(f.datum_key) AS erste_tag, MAX(f.datum_key) AS letzter_tag FROM dw.fact_erzeugung f JOIN dw.dim_energietraeger e ON e.energietraeger_key = f.energietraeger_key WHERE e.name_de = N'{et}' AND f.datum_key BETWEEN {von} AND {bis}) g CROSS APPLY (SELECT AVG(m.mw) AS mw FROM (SELECT k.datum_key, SUM(k.bestand_mw) AS mw FROM dw.fact_kapazitaet k JOIN dw.dim_energietraeger e2 ON e2.energietraeger_key = k.energietraeger_key WHERE e2.name_de = N'{et}' AND k.datum_key BETWEEN (g.erste_tag / 100) * 100 + 1 AND g.letzter_tag GROUP BY k.datum_key) m) c
- Anzahl Einheiten Zubau / Units commissioned [units]: Number of units commissioned in the months of the period. SQL: SELECT SUM(k.anzahl_zubau) FROM dw.fact_kapazitaet k WHERE k.datum_key BETWEEN {von} AND {bis}
- Datenstand / Data as of [timestamp (UTC)]: Newest loaded UTC timestamp of the generation data, independent of any filter. SQL: SELECT MAX(f.ts_utc) FROM dw.fact_erzeugung f
- Zeilen / Rows [rows]: Number of rows of the four fact tables (generation, load, price, capacity), for data-quality checks. SQL: SELECT (SELECT COUNT(*) FROM dw.fact_erzeugung WHERE datum_key BETWEEN {von} AND {bis}) + (SELECT COUNT(*) FROM dw.fact_last WHERE datum_key BETWEEN {von} AND {bis}) + (SELECT COUNT(*) FROM dw.fact_preis WHERE datum_key BETWEEN {von} AND {bis}) + (SELECT COUNT(*) FROM dw.fact_kapazitaet WHERE datum_key BETWEEN {von} AND {bis})

## Examples
Example 1
Frage: Gib für 2024 die monatliche Stromerzeugung aus Wasserkraft in DE-LU an (Monatsnummer und MWh), aufsteigend nach Monat.
Question: List the monthly electricity generation from hydro power in DE-LU for 2024 (month number and MWh), in ascending month order.
Answer:
```sql
SELECT d.monat, SUM(f.erzeugung_mwh) AS erzeugung_mwh
FROM dw.fact_erzeugung f
JOIN dw.dim_datum d ON d.datum_key = f.datum_key
JOIN dw.dim_energietraeger e ON e.energietraeger_key = f.energietraeger_key
JOIN dw.dim_region r ON r.region_key = f.region_key
WHERE r.smard_code = N'DE-LU' AND e.name_de = N'Wasserkraft' AND d.jahr = 2024
GROUP BY d.monat
ORDER BY d.monat
```

Example 2
Frage: Nenne die drei Bundesländer mit dem größten Wind-Onshore-Bestand Ende Dezember 2025 (Name und MW), größter zuerst.
Question: Name the three federal states with the largest onshore wind capacity at the end of December 2025 (name and MW), largest first.
Answer:
```sql
SELECT TOP 3 b.name_de, k.bestand_mw
FROM dw.fact_kapazitaet k
JOIN dw.dim_bundesland b ON b.bundesland_key = k.bundesland_key
JOIN dw.dim_energietraeger e ON e.energietraeger_key = k.energietraeger_key
WHERE e.name_de = N'Wind Onshore' AND k.datum_key = 20251201
ORDER BY k.bestand_mw DESC
```

Example 3
Frage: Welche Energiemenge in GWh lieferten Offshore-Windparks in DE-LU am Tag der Zeitumstellung im Frühjahr 2025 (30. März, deutsche Ortszeit)?
Question: How much energy in GWh did offshore wind farms deliver in DE-LU on the day of the spring clock change 2025 (30 March, German local time)?
Answer:
```sql
SELECT SUM(f.erzeugung_mwh) / 1000.0 AS erzeugung_gwh
FROM dw.fact_erzeugung f
JOIN dw.dim_energietraeger e ON e.energietraeger_key = f.energietraeger_key
JOIN dw.dim_region r ON r.region_key = f.region_key
WHERE r.smard_code = N'DE-LU' AND e.name_de = N'Wind Offshore' AND f.datum_key = 20250330
```

Example 4
Frage: In welcher ISO-Kalenderwoche des Jahres 2024 war der durchschnittliche Day-Ahead-Preis in DE-LU am niedrigsten? Gib Woche und Durchschnittspreis (€/MWh) aus; bei Gleichstand die frühere Woche.
Question: In which ISO calendar week of 2024 was the average day-ahead price in DE-LU lowest? Return the week and the average price (EUR/MWh); on a tie, the earlier week.
Answer:
```sql
SELECT TOP 1 d.iso_woche, AVG(p.day_ahead_eur_mwh) AS avg_preis_eur_mwh
FROM dw.fact_preis p
JOIN dw.dim_datum d ON d.datum_key = p.datum_key
JOIN dw.dim_region r ON r.region_key = p.region_key
WHERE r.smard_code = N'DE-LU' AND d.iso_jahr = 2024
GROUP BY d.iso_woche
ORDER BY avg_preis_eur_mwh ASC, d.iso_woche ASC
```

Example 5
Frage: Welcher Tag (Ortszeit) hatte in den Jahren 2023, 2024 und 2025 jeweils den höchsten Tagesdurchschnitt des Day-Ahead-Preises in DE-LU? Gib Jahr, Datum und Tagesdurchschnitt (€/MWh) aus; bei Gleichstand das frühere Datum.
Question: For each of the years 2023, 2024 and 2025, which day (local time) had the highest daily average day-ahead price in DE-LU? Return year, date and daily average (EUR/MWh); on a tie, the earlier date.
Answer:
```sql
WITH tag AS (
    SELECT d.jahr, d.datum, AVG(p.day_ahead_eur_mwh) AS avg_preis_eur_mwh
    FROM dw.fact_preis p
    JOIN dw.dim_datum d ON d.datum_key = p.datum_key
    JOIN dw.dim_region r ON r.region_key = p.region_key
    WHERE r.smard_code = N'DE-LU' AND d.jahr BETWEEN 2023 AND 2025
    GROUP BY d.jahr, d.datum
), rangiert AS (
    SELECT jahr, datum, avg_preis_eur_mwh,
           ROW_NUMBER() OVER (PARTITION BY jahr ORDER BY avg_preis_eur_mwh DESC, datum ASC) AS rn
    FROM tag
)
SELECT jahr, datum, avg_preis_eur_mwh FROM rangiert WHERE rn = 1 ORDER BY jahr
```
